About Our VTC

Welcome to Vortex Logistics—a premier Virtual Trucking Company (VTC) operating across the expansive highways of Euro Truck Simulator 2. Founded on a passion for authentic simulation and community camaraderie, we are dedicated to setting the gold standard for virtual transport solutions. At Vortex Logistics, we blend realistic convoy operations with a welcoming, driver-first environment. Whether you are a seasoned driver logging thousands of kilometers a week or a newcomer looking to learn the ropes of long-haul trucking, Vortex Logistics provides the ultimate platform to elevate your driving experience.
